What we do

Four trades, one contractor

Insulation, surface treatment, scaffolding and passive fire protection share the same work fronts. Running them under one team means fewer interfaces, one safety system and one schedule.

Insulation

Fabrication and installation of insulation systems for hot, cold and cryogenic service.

  • Thermal and acoustic
  • Cryogenic and LNG
  • Fire-rated and personnel protection

Surface treatment & coating

On-site surface preparation, protective coating and inspection to stop corrosion at the source.

  • Blasting and surface preparation
  • Protective coating systems
  • SSPC-qualified inspection

Access & scaffolding

Scaffolding planned around insulation and coating work, so access is ready when the trades need it.

  • MoM-certified scaffolders
  • 15,000 sq ft scaffold yard
  • Plant shutdowns and maintenance

Passive fire protection

Approved applicator for leading PFP manufacturers on structural steel and equipment.

  • Intumescent PFP
  • Cementitious PFP
  • Onshore and offshore

About Everstar

A Singapore contractor for process and offshore plants since 2008

Everstar Engineering & Services and its three subsidiaries deliver insulation, coating, access and fire protection across Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia.

Company profile

Turnkey supply and install, with our own engineering

We take responsibility for the whole non-mechanical package: design, materials, fabrication, access and installation. That means one contract, one safety system and one team accountable for the result.

  • Good HSE track record
  • Skilled, experienced staff at competitive prices
  • Strong network for sourcing materials
  • Process (onshore) and marine (offshore) work
Milestones
  1. 2008Everstar Engineering & Services Pte Ltd established in Singapore.

  3. 2025More than 100 projects delivered and 530+ people across Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia.

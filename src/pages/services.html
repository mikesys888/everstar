Engineering services

Corrosion under insulation (CUI), designed out

CUI starts when the coating, insulation and jacketing are chosen separately. We specify them as one system with the coating and insulation manufacturers, then install it to a method that protects its service life.

  1. 1
    Anti-corrosion coatingSelected for operating temperature and cyclic wet/dry exposure.
  2. 2
    InsulationMaterial and thickness optimised to the process requirement, including LNG and cryogenic duty.
  3. 3
    Jacketing & sealingMetal cladding fabricated in our own workshop and sealed to keep water out.
